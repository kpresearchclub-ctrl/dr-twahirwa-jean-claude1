Professional Biography

Dr. TWAHIRWA JEAN CLAUDE is an academic professional associated with Kibogora Polytechnic, Rwanda. His professional interests include nursing education, scientific research, healthcare improvement, academic leadership and professional development.

His academic and research interests focus on strengthening the relationship between scientific evidence, healthcare practice and education. Through research and academic engagement, he contributes to discussions addressing healthcare challenges and the development of effective, evidence-informed approaches to patient care.

His research interests include palliative care, pain management, informal home-based caregiving, nursing practice and public health. He is also interested in supporting students and emerging researchers in developing research skills and communicating scientific findings.

Through academic collaboration, mentorship and professional consultancy, he seeks to contribute to the development of knowledge, improvement of healthcare services and advancement of nursing education in Rwanda and beyond.
